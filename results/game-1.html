  4. 41 run of 7

Scoring

Cards 1–95 each
Cards 10–1210 each
Skip15
Wild25
  • Set: same number, any colors.
  • Run: numbers in order, any colors. No wrapping from 12 to 1.
  • One phase per hand. Lay it down, then hit on any laid-down phase.
  • First to finish Phase 10 wins. Ties go to fewer points.
